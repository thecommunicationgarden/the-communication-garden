A little about me

Hello, I'm Lucy

I'm an independent speech and language therapist with 13 years of experience supporting children and young people across special schools, mainstream schools and clinical settings.

I'm passionate about helping children communicate in ways that work for them. Every child and family is different, so I take time to understand each child's strengths, interests and needs before shaping support around them.

Working in partnership with families and the people who support a child is at the heart of my practice. Together, we can build strategies that make a meaningful difference in everyday life.

  • HCPC registered speech and language therapist
  • Member of the Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists (RCSLT)
How I can help

Support that starts with the child

I support children and young people with a range of communication needs, tailoring therapy to what matters most to them and their family.

01

Language difficulties

Support for children who find it difficult to understand language, express their ideas, learn new words or put words and sentences together, including children with Developmental Language Disorder (DLD).

02

Autism & social communication

Individualised support for autistic children and children with social communication differences, respecting each child's communication style, preferences and strengths.

03

Communication and emotional wellbeing

Thoughtful support for children with social, emotional and mental health (SEMH) needs, recognising the relationship between communication, connection and wellbeing.

My approach

Therapy for real life

Communication happens everywhere — at home, at school, with friends and during the little moments of everyday life.

“The best support grows from understanding the child, working together and focusing on what makes a difference to them.”

I aim to make therapy practical, functional and enjoyable. I work collaboratively with families, carers and education staff so that strategies can be used in the environments that matter to the child.

Flexible support

Support where you need it

I offer home and education-setting visits across Worthing and the surrounding areas, alongside online therapy.

I aim to make therapy as accessible and practical as possible, working with children and their families in the environments where communication matters most.
